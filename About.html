Hapag Tanaw was founded in 1956 in San Jose del Monte, Bulacan, with a simple yet heartfelt vision: to bring people together through the timeless flavors of Filipino cuisine. What began as a small family passion project quickly grew into a modern dining destination that celebrates both heritage and innovation. For nearly seven decades, we have remained committed to preserving the authentic tastes that define Filipino cooking while embracing contemporary culinary techniques. Our kitchen serves as a bridge between generations, where age-old recipes passed down through our family are reimagined with fresh, locally-sourced ingredients and modern presentation. Each dish tells a story—of family gatherings, festive celebrations, and the warmth of shared meals that have brought communities together for centuries. At Hapag Tanaw, dining is more than just a meal; it's an experience that honors our roots while looking toward the future. From our signature adobo, slow-cooked to perfection with a recipe that has remained unchanged since our founding, to innovative interpretations of classic Filipino comfort food, every plate reflects our dedication to quality, tradition, and the joy of bringing people together. We invite you to join us at our table, where every visit becomes part of our continuing legacy—a legacy built on love, flavor, and the enduring spirit of Filipino hospitality.

Born in the year 1886 to a humble farming family, Lola Fely grew up in a home where food was more than just sustenance—it was an expression of care, patience, and unconditional love. From a young age, she learned the value of hard work in the fields and the importance of preparing meals that brought comfort after long days under the sun. Every dish she cooked carried stories of resilience, tradition, and family, passed down through generations. As the years went by, Lola Fely became widely known in her community for her remarkable cooking. Her slow-cooked adobo, simmered with care until the flavors were perfectly balanced, and her crisp, golden lumpia, prepared with meticulous attention to detail, became staples at fiestas, family reunions, and neighborhood celebrations.

As the years passed and Lola Fely grew older, her children naturally became the keepers of both her treasured family recipes and the values she lived by. Under her guidance, they learned not only how to cook each dish with care and precision, but also how to infuse every meal with love, patience, and respect for tradition. When the time came for them to take over Hapag Tanaw, they embraced the responsibility with pride, understanding that the restaurant was more than a business—it was their mother’s lifelong dream and legacy. With a new generation at the helm, the children thoughtfully modernized the atmosphere of Hapag Tanaw, creating a space that felt warm, welcoming, and suited to contemporary dining while still honoring its humble roots.
